CLUES FROM A ROYAL TOMB

PHOTO INVESTIGATION · 6 ARTIFACTS

Look closely.
What can you infer?

Investigate six artifacts from your King Tut assignment. Start with what you can see, then explain what the evidence suggests about the people who made and used each object.

Select a photograph to enlarge it. Record your observations and explanations in your class assignment.

Build your explanation.

1. Meet the artifact

What materials might it be made from? Point to visible details that support your choices.

2. Observe its parts

Describe its shape, color, texture, condition, markings and possible moving parts. Write so someone who cannot see it could imagine it.

3. Make sense of it

Who might have used it? What might it have been used for? What can it tell us about the people who made and used it?

FOR THE CLASSROOM

Observe first.
Then check your ideas.
