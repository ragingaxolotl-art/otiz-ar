The article’s full 15-category checklist

Category names follow the article you shared. Its descriptions are not our historical authority; object identity and placement should follow museum and Griffith Institute records. Searches checked on 14 November 2026. An unfilled row means no sufficiently specific free reusable mesh was verified in this search, not that none exists anywhere.

  1. Canopic jars

    The canopic chest by danderson4 is already saved (CC BY-NC 4.0). It is not a complete set of removable jars, lids and miniature coffins. Tutankhamun Coffinettes is a further viewable lead; download/license not verified.

  2. Senet game

    Teacher priority 04 above; generic model available, exact board missing.

  3. Personal fan

    Teacher priority 03 above; exact artifact missing.

  4. Anubis statue and shrine

    Two relevant models and their restrictions are recorded below. The photographed original is view-only; the free replica is not cleared for hosting on our site.

  5. Leopard head

    No free downloadable model of the specific tomb object verified. A generic animal head would misrepresent the artifact.

  6. Sun scarab pendant

    Mayantique’s artistic pectoral is now uploaded; see above. The exact pendant in the article must be matched against museum records; Tut had several distinct scarab pectorals.

  7. Burial mask

    Roberto Domínguez mask already saved · CC BY 4.0. No need to download another mask first.

  8. Throwing sticks

    Teacher priority 01 above; matching model missing.

  9. Statue of Ptah

    No verified free mesh of Tut’s specific figure found. Egypt Centre Ptah-Sokar-Osiris W2059 is a different museum object, not a replacement for Tut’s figure.

  10. Royal chariot

    Teacher priority 06 above; relevant view-only lead, no verified free mesh.

  11. Perfume vessels

    Cults alabaster perfume vase and 3DExport vase are commercial reconstruction leads, not free downloads. Exact object fidelity and public website redistribution rights need checking.

  12. Personal necessities

    Existing throne reproduction, study bed and headrest comparison cover only part of this broad category. Golden sandals is a viewable lead, download/license unverified. Jewelry candidate above; other exact collars, clothing and sandals remain open.

  13. Painting of warfare

    Use the painted chest already on site. The article’s heading does not establish a separate freestanding painting to add. Do not build a crossbow from the article’s description.

  14. Tutankhamun as a child

    The lotus head by danderson4 is already saved; details below. This is preferable to a generic child-pharaoh bust.

  15. Mummified body

    No verified free downloadable model of Tutankhamun’s actual mummified remains identified. Griffith archival account is a research reference. Generic mummies should not be labeled Tutankhamun.

Additional leads to keep separate

Tutankhamun’s trumpet [FREE] by sergeilihandristov has no Sketchfab Download button. Its author directs downloads to Telegram and acknowledges an inaccurate pattern; reuse license is not stated on the page. Treat as a lead, not an approved free asset.

Detail of the Throne of Tutankhamun by Digor appears as downloadable in search results, but is only a detail and its license has not been verified. Lower priority than our complete replica.

Do not add as tomb finds: Harvard’s generic headrest, Tutankhamun temple statue fragments, the Luxor temple dyad, objects belonging to Yuya/Tjuyu, or other people’s coffins. Tutankhamun in a title does not prove a KV62 findspot.

Missing tomb models: search results

Anubis shrine: Scan the World’s replica model has a free Download button. Its current license includes a restriction against hosting the design on another website, so it is not cleared for embedding as a local model in our public site. It is a scan of a modern replica, not the ancient original. danderson4’s photographed original is viewable, but no Download button is offered.

Guardian statues: Arqueoartis’s Ka statue is a relevant museum model, but no Download button is offered. No free downloadable model of the correct pair was verified.

Four nested burial shrines: no free, reusable downloadable set verified. These are distinct from the canopic shrine and Anubis shrine. Carter’s fourth-shrine documentation provides a reconstruction reference, not a ready-made mesh.

Couches: the free Ioan model below is now saved and shown as a study reconstruction. The leg-count error remains visible and labeled; it does not supply three accurate couch models.

Tutankhamun mannequin

Open model and download →

By Roberto Domínguez · CC BY 4.0 · about 139,200 triangles. Sketchfab labels this model “Generated with AI.” Use only as a labeled approximation, not a museum scan.

The ancient wooden portrait figure, Carter 116, is documented from the tomb. Its purpose as a clothes or jewelry mannequin is a suggestion, not a certainty. Verify the generated shape against photographs before placement. Archive record · ARCE explanation.

ALREADY SAVED · NEEDS CORRECTION

Funerary bed of Tutankamon

Open model and download →

By Ioan / ioancroitor · CC BY 4.0 · about 787,400 triangles. A hand-made reconstruction. The creator explicitly notes incorrect leg count and recommends reducing the mesh for game use.

Animal-headed funerary couches belonged in Tutankhamun’s antechamber, but this mesh needs comparison with the numbered couch records and corrections before it represents a specific couch. Useful as a starting asset, not ready for an accuracy-focused scene. Carter’s throne card mentions couch 137.
