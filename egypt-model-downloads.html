By danderson4 · CC BY-NC 4.0 · about 288,700 triangles. The creator describes a model made from 141 photographs.

The actual object is Carter 8, associated with the tomb’s entrance corridor. The Griffith Institute describes this as a reported findspot, so an exact coordinate should remain uncertain. Excavation reference.

Tutankhamun mannequin

Open model and download →

By Roberto Domínguez · CC BY 4.0 · about 139,200 triangles. Sketchfab labels this model “Generated with AI.” Use only as a labeled approximation, not a museum scan.

The ancient wooden portrait figure, Carter 116, is documented from the tomb. Its purpose as a clothes or jewelry mannequin is a suggestion, not a certainty. Verify the generated shape against photographs before placement. Archive record · ARCE explanation.

DOWNLOADABLE · NEEDS CORRECTION

Funerary bed of Tutankamon

Open model and download →

By Ioan / ioancroitor · CC BY 4.0 · about 787,400 triangles. A hand-made reconstruction. The creator explicitly notes incorrect leg count and recommends reducing the mesh for game use.

Animal-headed funerary couches belonged in Tutankhamun’s antechamber, but this mesh needs comparison with the numbered couch records and corrections before it represents a specific couch. Useful as a starting asset, not ready for an accuracy-focused scene. Carter’s throne card mentions couch 137.

HOLD · PROVENANCE NOT ESTABLISHED

Anubis study

View the candidate by Lova Dahn · CC BY 4.0 · free download shown. The description only says it is a quick study of Anubis. A Tutankhamun tag does not establish a faithful model of his Anubis shrine. Do not place it as the excavated shrine without a visual comparison and provenance check.

Accuracy priority: the four burial shrines, guardian statues, correct funerary couches, Anubis shrine and canopic canopy will change the room’s appearance most.
