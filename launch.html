Start exploring

  1. Tap Start camera and allow camera access.
  2. Point the iPad at one numbered floor card. Keep its full black border in view.
  3. Move around slowly to investigate the 3D artifact. Keep the card visible.
  4. Collect evidence: what materials, skills, and daily needs does the artifact suggest?
