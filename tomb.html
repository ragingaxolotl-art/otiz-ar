HOW TO EXPLORE

Controls & collection

Keyboard & mouse

Double-tap forward: tap, release, then press forward again within 0.3 seconds and hold for 2× speed. Release to walk normally. Works with touch, W, left stick or D-pad; ZL precision movement overrides the boost. Touch: drag the scene to look around; use the movement arrows and Pick up button. Keyboard: WASD moves; arrow keys also look. E picks up/returns, Q puts back, C toggles crouch, and R returns to the stairs. Hold Space to crouch; releasing keeps you crouched. A short Space tap while crouched only stands up. After you are fully standing, another short tap jumps. Low ceilings prevent standing and jumping.

Mouse: move the mouse to look after choosing Mouse look; Esc releases the cursor. Left-click the scene to pick up the targeted artifact (or return the held copy); right-click puts it back. While holding an artifact, mouse movement rotates it; scroll the wheel to bring it closer or farther away. Click the wheel (middle-click) to reset the artifact view, or to recenter when empty-handed. If mouse capture is unavailable, hold either mouse button and drag to look or rotate. Touch dragging still works.

About this space

The tomb is a digital reconstruction by akurtulus. Object placement follows documented rooms where possible. Teaching stands expose burial objects that were originally concealed inside coffins and wrappings. The tomb’s modern wall images show aged surfaces, not proven original colors. Teaching ramps bridge floor drops at the burial chamber and Annexe; these are modern access aids. Crouch to pass low doorways. Jump to reach ledges about one meter high when there is headroom. Walk or jump off an edge to drop and land below; ramps remain optional access aids. Picking up an object shows a scaled digital copy; returning it restores its exhibit position. The combined souvenir display has been separated: its coffin sits inside the stone sarcophagus, with the head west, and its Anubis shrine is near the Treasury entrance, also facing west. Both are replicas. Its unidentified chair/vessel component is retained only as reference. The pectoral is on a modern study stand; its exact findspot is unverified.

Entrance stairs

The walkthrough starts on the top landing, facing down sixteen teaching steps into the descending corridor. The staircase and narrow opening through the source entrance are navigation adaptations, not a surveyed reconstruction. Return to stairs (controller + or keyboard R) brings you back to this landing.

Model credits

    Walkthrough models use mobile meshes. Sharper source color textures (2048–4096 pixels) load automatically when an artifact is targeted or held. Only one artifact uses the extra detail at a time. Textures above 4096 pixels are reduced; opaque PNG color images are JPEG-compressed at quality 95. Original model links and licenses are preserved in the asset manifest.
