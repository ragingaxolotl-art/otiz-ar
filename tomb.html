HOW TO EXPLORE

Controls & collection

Screen controls

F — Full screen. Press again to exit. If your iPhone browser keeps its bars visible, the button explains how to open the tomb from your Home Screen. H — Hide / show interface. Hides menus, detailed status and action buttons; direction arrows, screen controls and compact bottom hints stay visible. Keyboard, mouse and controller actions still work.

Keyboard & mouse

Double-tap forward: tap, release, then press forward again within 0.3 seconds and hold for 2× speed. Release to walk normally. Works with touch, W, left stick or D-pad; ZL precision movement overrides the boost. Touch: drag the scene to look around; use the movement arrows and Pick up button. Keyboard: WASD moves; arrow keys also look. E picks up/returns, Q puts back, C toggles crouch, Z/X rotate the held object left/right, and R returns to the stairs. Hold Space to crouch; releasing keeps you crouched. A short Space tap while crouched only stands up. After you are fully standing, another short tap jumps. Low ceilings prevent standing and jumping.

Mouse: move the mouse to look after choosing Mouse look; Esc releases the cursor. A short left-click picks up the targeted artifact for rotation. Click Collect, or press E / controller A while inspecting, to add it to the book. Q / controller B returns it. Press Collect while inspecting to add it to the book; inspection alone never collects it. In Cardboard mode, hold left-click to walk forward; move the mouse while holding to steer, then release to stop. In landscape mode, mouse holds do not move you; use WASD, the movement arrows or a controller. Hold right-click and drag to look without walking; a short right-click jumps when empty-handed or returns the held artifact. Left-drag turns a carried artifact horizontally. Middle-button dragging rotates it freely. These gestures also work with a press-and-hold on the scene when iPhone treats the pointer as touch. When empty-handed, scroll to change view size; scroll up for a closer view and down for a wider view. In View & lenses, the wheel adjusts the row under the pointer. Over the center dots it adjusts screen spacing, moving both complete views together or apart. While holding an artifact, left-drag turns it horizontally and middle-drag rotates it freely; scroll the wheel to bring it closer or farther away. Zoom stops before the artifact surface reaches your eyes; turning the object adjusts that limit too. When empty-handed, click the wheel to reset view size and recenter. While carrying an artifact, middle-button dragging rotates it. If mouse capture is unavailable, drag to look, or middle-drag to rotate a held artifact. Touch dragging still works.

Cardboard: choose Cardboard view, allow motion access on the iPhone, then place the phone in the viewer horizontally. Look at a nearby object and press A to pick it up. Stay seated; movement is controlled by the stick or keyboard. Artifact information and control hints appear at the bottom of both eye views. Use View & lenses to change view size, lens correction, eye spacing, image position or swap eyes without leaving the viewer. Settings are saved on this device. Save fit returns to walking. With a mouse, hold left-click on the scene to walk and move it to steer; release to stop. Back, Crouch and Jump buttons let you explore without a keyboard. Hide hints leaves only the small View & lenses, Exit VR and Show hints toolbar. It does not change the regular-screen interface. Press H, the controller − button, or Show hints to restore the VR hints. View & lenses remains available while hints are hidden.

Adjust for your viewer. Phone/browser motion support and controller mappings vary; use landscape view if head tracking is unavailable.

Flashlight

The tomb is deliberately dark. Your flashlight follows your view in landscape and Cardboard modes. Press L or click the light button to cycle Wide / weak, Medium, and Small / strong. The small strong beam is the original light and is selected when the tomb opens.

About this space

The tomb is a digital reconstruction by akurtulus. Object placement follows documented rooms where possible. Modern exhibit stands have been removed. Burial objects associated with the mummy are grouped with the coffin; other teaching copies rest at floor level in their documented comparison rooms. Exact coordinates remain approximate. The tomb’s modern wall images show aged surfaces, not proven original colors. Teaching ramps bridge floor drops at the burial chamber and Annexe; these are modern access aids. The player ducks automatically when a low doorway blocks standing movement; press C to stand once clear. Jump to reach ledges about one meter high when there is headroom. Walk or jump off an edge to drop and land below; ramps are enabled by default and can be switched off in these controls. Picking up an object shows a scaled digital copy; returning it restores its exhibit position. The crook and flail are now displayed on the wrapped teaching mummy inside the openable inner coffin; the earlier loose display copy has been removed from the room. Four Meshy shrine reconstructions surround the stone sarcophagus and three nested coffins, with the coffin heads west. Use Explore burial layers to reveal each enclosure. The old souvenir coffin has been removed from the game. The Anubis shrine replica remains near the Treasury entrance, facing west. The pectoral is a floor-level Treasury comparison; its exact findspot is unverified.

Entrance stairs

The walkthrough starts on the top landing, facing down sixteen teaching steps into the descending corridor. The staircase and narrow opening through the source entrance are navigation adaptations, not a surveyed reconstruction. Return to stairs (controller + or keyboard R) brings you back to this landing.

Player body

The Adventurer uses the supplied character’s original rig and animations. The first-person defaults match the approved camera settings. Head and torso are hidden in the running view; look down to see the moving arms and legs. The source model is preserved.
