HOW TO EXPLORE

Controls & collection

Screen controls

F — Full screen. Press again to exit. If your iPhone browser keeps its bars visible, the button explains how to open the tomb from your Home Screen. H — Hide / show interface. Hides menus, detailed status and action buttons; direction arrows, screen controls and compact bottom hints stay visible. Keyboard, mouse and controller actions still work.

Keyboard & mouse

Double-tap forward: tap, release, then press forward again within 0.3 seconds and hold for 2× speed. Release to walk normally. Works with touch, W, left stick or D-pad; ZL precision movement overrides the boost. Touch: drag the scene to look around; use the movement arrows and Pick up button. Keyboard: WASD moves; arrow keys also look. E picks up/returns, Q puts back, C toggles crouch, Z/X rotate the held object left/right, and R returns to the stairs. Hold Space to crouch; releasing keeps you crouched. A short Space tap while crouched only stands up. After you are fully standing, another short tap jumps. Low ceilings prevent standing and jumping.

Mouse: move the mouse to look after choosing Mouse look; Esc releases the cursor. A short left-click on the scene picks up the targeted artifact (or returns the held copy). Hold left-click to walk forward; move the mouse while holding to steer, then release to stop. Hold right-click and drag to look without walking; a short right-click jumps when empty-handed or returns the held artifact. Holding a mouse button while carrying an artifact rotates it instead of walking. These gestures also work with a press-and-hold on the scene when iPhone treats the pointer as touch. When empty-handed, scroll to change view size; scroll up for a closer view and down for a wider view. In View & lenses, the wheel adjusts the row under the pointer. Over the center dots it adjusts screen spacing, moving both complete views together or apart. While holding an artifact, mouse movement rotates it; scroll the wheel to bring it closer or farther away. Zoom stops before the artifact surface reaches your eyes; turning the object adjusts that limit too. Click the wheel (middle-click) to reset the artifact view, or to reset view size and recenter when empty-handed. If mouse capture is unavailable, hold either mouse button and drag to look or rotate. Touch dragging still works.

Cardboard: choose Cardboard view, allow motion access on the iPhone, then place the phone in the viewer horizontally. Look at a nearby object and press A to pick it up. Stay seated; movement is controlled by the stick or keyboard. Artifact information and control hints appear at the bottom of both eye views. Use View & lenses to change view size, lens correction, eye spacing, image position or swap eyes without leaving the viewer. Settings are saved on this device. Save fit returns to walking. With a mouse, hold left-click on the scene to walk and move it to steer; release to stop. Back, Crouch and Jump buttons let you explore without a keyboard. Hide hints leaves only the small View & lenses, Exit VR and Show hints toolbar. It does not change the regular-screen interface. Press H, the controller − button, or Show hints to restore the VR hints. View & lenses remains available while hints are hidden.

Adjust for your viewer. Phone/browser motion support and controller mappings vary; use landscape view if head tracking is unavailable.

About this space

The tomb is a digital reconstruction by akurtulus. Object placement follows documented rooms where possible. Teaching stands expose burial objects that were originally concealed inside coffins and wrappings. The tomb’s modern wall images show aged surfaces, not proven original colors. Teaching ramps bridge floor drops at the burial chamber and Annexe; these are modern access aids. Crouch to pass low doorways. Jump to reach ledges about one meter high when there is headroom. Walk or jump off an edge to drop and land below; ramps remain optional access aids. Picking up an object shows a scaled digital copy; returning it restores its exhibit position. The combined souvenir display has been separated: its coffin sits inside the stone sarcophagus, with the head west, and its Anubis shrine is near the Treasury entrance, also facing west. Both are replicas. Its unidentified chair/vessel component is retained only as reference. The pectoral is on a modern study stand; its exact findspot is unverified.

Entrance stairs

The walkthrough starts on the top landing, facing down sixteen teaching steps into the descending corridor. The staircase and narrow opening through the source entrance are navigation adaptations, not a surveyed reconstruction. Return to stairs (controller + or keyboard R) brings you back to this landing.

Player body

The Girl explorer body follows your movement in first person. Look down to see it. It is scaled to the existing 1.75 m player reference, with eyes at 1.60 m; those are teaching scale settings, not a measured real person. Head and hair are hidden from the first-person camera. Damaged clothing/accessory meshes are omitted; brown shorts and boot colors are applied to the body instead. The original model is preserved. Walking, running and jumping use its animations; crouching lowers the body reference with the camera.
